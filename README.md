# Indian-Railway-Data-Analysis
Indian Railways Train Delay Analysis using Microsoft Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

The Indian Railway Data Analysis Project focuses on analyzing train delays, station performance, and delay reasons using Microsoft Excel.

The project uses railway data to identify delay patterns, compare train types, analyze station performance, and present meaningful insights through an Excel dashboard, analytical report, and PowerPoint presentation.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze train arrival and departure delays.
* Identify trains with the highest average arrival delays.
* Compare train delays across different train types.
* Identify stations with the highest average arrival delays.
* Analyze the major reasons for train delays.
* Compare delay patterns by day of the week and month.
* Present findings through reports, charts, and a dashboard.

## 📊 Dataset Overview

The project presentation reports the following dataset statistics:

* **Total Records:** 91,133
* **Different Trains:** 16
* **Railway Stations:** 190
* **Train Types:** 4

The dataset includes train information, station codes, distances, scheduled and actual timings, arrival and departure delays, delay reasons, and date-related information.

## 📈 Key Analysis Performed

### 1. Train Delay Analysis

Analyzed average arrival and departure delays and identified the maximum recorded delays.

### 2. Train Performance Analysis

Compared trains based on their average arrival delays and identified the most delayed trains.

### 3. Station Performance Analysis

Analyzed station-wise average arrival delays to identify stations with higher recorded delays.

### 4. Delay Reason Analysis

Examined arrival and departure delay reasons, including signal issues, engine issues, late-running preceding trains, and track maintenance.

### 5. Day-Wise Analysis

Compared average train delays across the days of the week.

### 6. Month-Wise Analysis

Analyzed monthly arrival and departure delay patterns to identify periods with higher average delays.

### 7. Train-Type Analysis

Compared average delays across different train categories.

## 💡 Key Findings

According to the project analysis:

* Average arrival delay was approximately **124.68 minutes**.
* Average departure delay was approximately **116.25 minutes**.
* The maximum recorded arrival delay was **3,464 minutes**.
* The maximum recorded departure delay was **3,209 minutes**.
* Track maintenance was the most frequently recorded departure delay reason.
* Monday had the highest average arrival delay among the days analyzed.
* November had the highest average arrival delay among the months analyzed.
* Train 1030 had the highest average arrival delay among the trains shown in the analysis.

These findings describe the analyzed dataset and should not be interpreted as current statistics for the entire Indian Railways
